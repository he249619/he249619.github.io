# Fast Robots

ECE 4160: Fast Robots is a lab-based course at Cornell University that focuses on autonomous, dynamic, mobile, and fast robot development through a systems level design. We work on integrating hardware as well as developing software to create and control a fast robot car. We delve into a wide spread of topics, including wired and wireless communication protocols, PID and LQR control, mapping, localization, Kalman and Bayes filtering, and stunt performance. My particular favorite labs were the Mapping and the Inverted Pendulum Stunt labs.

Please visit [here](https://hilarioesparza.github.io/fastrobots-2026/) to see my work and documentation for this course.
