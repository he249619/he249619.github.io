## [ECE 4160: Fast Robots](https://hilarioesparza.github.io/fastrobots-2026/)

This lab-based class focused on the hardware and software development and debugging process of a robotic car. We took the electrical guts out of a toy RC car, rebuilt it with our own system, and then programmed it to perform mapping, localization, Kalman filter aided position and orientation PID control, and cool stunts! My particular favorite part of the class was working on the inverted pendulum challenge in the last lab.
